Lessons from the PEC audit

Separate the trace before interpreting the user.

01

Do not collapse P, E, S, and C.

Stated preference, logged lists, interface pathways, and clicks are different measurements. Agreement between two layers does not make them interchangeable.

02

Name the missing mechanism.

A logged item does not establish visual attention. A click does not establish reading depth. A same-day match does not establish that the recommendation caused the click.

03

Make the denominator part of the result.

Count matching, click thresholds, retained-list coverage, and eligible-user rules can change the apparent gap. Report them beside every metric.

04

Treat absence as bounded uncertainty.

Not finding a click in one retained list means “not traceable here.” It does not prove that the platform never surfaced the article elsewhere.

The paper’s message: responsible recommender evaluation begins by distinguishing what the system recorded, what the analyst inferred, and what the data cannot reveal.

When Exposure
Is Not Attention

Auditing the Preference–Exposure–Consumption Gap
in Personalized News Recommenders

Woojin Park · Korea University
Preprint · soon Code & artifacts Author page

A measurement audit for separating what users say, what systems log, and what people click.

The framework treats preference, logged exposure, surface mediation, and click consumption as distinct observable layers rather than a single sequential pipeline.

01 · Overview

One platform can tell four different stories.

Personalized news systems are often evaluated as if stated preferences, logged recommendation exposure, and click consumption were interchangeable. They are not. Each trace answers a different question, uses a different eligible denominator, and supports a different class of claims.

PEC is a relation-specific audit protocol. It connects preference (P), logged exposure (E), interface surfaces (S), and consumption (C) while making the platform's observability boundaries explicit.

02 · Audit protocol

Separate the layers before comparing them.

Every output is tied to a relation, a denominator, a validity control, and a bounded interpretation.

P

Preference

Stated categories, preferred sources, and the observed weighted profile state.

E

Logged exposure

User-date ranked lists and item-level recommendation records available in the logs.

S

Surface context

Headline, category, home/feed, newsroom, article detail, search, and other entry routes.

C

Consumption

Clicked articles and their category, publisher, time, and surface metadata.

2E ↔ CTrace clicks to same-day lists, exposure history, candidate-pool baselines, and popularity-aware baselines.Exposure–consumption traceability
2E ↔ CCount-match exposure to clicks; compute entropy, HHI, top share, effective categories, and JS divergence.Diversity shift
2P, E, CFit bounded diagnostic models excluding direct alignment quantities.Structured divergence
3OutputPair each empirical claim with its observability boundary.Audit report

Note. Protocol 1 separates what is observed, what is compared, and what remains outside the observable scope of the logs.

Boundary: PEC is a measurement protocol. It is not a ranking objective, a causal estimate, or a production targeting procedure.

Table 2: Metrics used to operationalize the PEC audit
Audit quantityOperational equation
Category Jaccard\(J_u=\dfrac{|P^{cat}_u\cap C^{top}_{cat}(u)|}{|P^{cat}_u\cup C^{top}_{cat}(u)|}\)
Divergence\(D_u=1-J_u\)
Preference-click cosine\(A_u=\cos(p^{cat}_{u,P},p^{cat}_{u,C})\)
Same-day hit\(H_{ui}=\mathbf{1}\{i\in E(u,\operatorname{date}(i))\}\)
Top-\(k\) lift\(\mathrm{Lift}_k=\dfrac{\mathbb{E}[H^k_{obs}]}{\mathbb{E}[H^k_{base}]}\)
Normalized entropy\(H_{\mathrm{norm}}(X)=-\sum_xp_x\log(p_x)/\log|X^+|,\quad X^+=\{x:p_x>0\}\)
HHI / top share\(\mathrm{HHI}(X)=\sum_xp_x^2,\qquad \mathrm{TopShare}(X)=\max_xp_x\)
JS divergence\(\mathrm{JS}_2(p,q)=\tfrac12\mathrm{KL}_2(p\parallel m)+\tfrac12\mathrm{KL}_2(q\parallel m),\quad m=\tfrac{p+q}{2}\)
Count-matched narrowing\(\Delta H_{\mathrm{norm}}=H_{\mathrm{norm}}(E'_u)-H_{\mathrm{norm}}(C_u);\; \Delta\mathrm{HHI}=\mathrm{HHI}(C_u)-\mathrm{HHI}(E'_u);\; |E'_u|=|C_u|\)

Note. \(C^{top}_{cat}(u)\) is the user's top three clicked categories, or fewer when fewer are observed. \(E'_u\) is sampled from the user's logged-exposure union. PEC reports divergence, not its square-root distance.

03 · Findings

Four views of the same system.

The conclusions change with the layer being measured.

RQ1 · Preference

Stated preferences are informative, but incomplete.

The top consumed category appears in stated interests for 77.22% of eligible users, above chance-based nulls. Mean category Jaccard is only 0.228, showing that dominant interests do not capture the broader consumption set.

77.22% top-category match0.228 mean Jaccard

RQ2 · Traceability

Logged recommendation exposure carries signal, not complete attention.

Only 11.29% of comparable clicks appear in the same-day logged recommendation list, versus a 9.13% date-matched candidate-pool expectation. Top-5 lift reaches 1.47×, while most clicks arrive through other surfaces.

11.29% observed hit1.47× top-5 lift

RQ3 · Diversity

Diversity depends on the measurement layer.

Logged exposure is broader than click consumption in the raw logs. Count matching removes much of the entropy gap, yet the audit-eligible cohort retains an HHI gap of 0.082 and JS divergence of 0.471.

0.082 HHI gap0.471 JS divergence

RQ4 · Divergence

The gap is structured, but the signal is bounded.

Same-window diagnostic AUC reaches 0.859 with weighted profile-state features. It falls to 0.731 in the time-split diagnostic and below 0.71 in denser-history cohorts, limiting operational interpretation.

0.859 same-window AUC0.731 time-split AUC
04 · Reproducibility

Reproducible where the data can safely travel.

The public artifact exposes metric implementations, aggregate tables, synthetic schemas, figure-generation code, and authorized rerun instructions without redistributing private behavioral traces.

Open repository Reproducibility notes
Public

Audit metrics, release-safe aggregate tables, synthetic examples, notebooks, and figure builders.

Protected

Raw operational logs, row-level click histories, recommendation lists, user profiles, and model feature rows.

Interpretation

Observational measurement claims only; no causal ranking effect, verified impression, or total-attention claim.
